The desk

How allocation actually works

Cigar Concierge is a person with a factory, a vault, and a DM inbox — not a shopping-cart app. This page is the protocol.

01

Browse

House blends have posted prices. Vault pieces are inquire-only because the box may already be spoken for.

02

Request

Add sticks to the list and send it on X. Name the cigar, the format, and how many you want.

03

Confirm

The Concierge replies with what’s left, the real number, and ship-to details. Vault pricing is quoted then, not guessed here.

04

Smoke

Aged Dominican house blends for Tuesdays. Opus and Davidoff for the nights that deserve them.

A good first message

“Need a box of Blockbuster and a bundle of The 250. Ship to Seattle. Also — any 25th Opus left?”
